05 · Payment Q&A
支付问答 + 快速操作
Q1: Can I use my foreign Visa / Mastercard?+
Yes. Both Alipay and WeChat Pay support international Visa and Mastercard. Link your card during setup and you can pay immediately.
Q2: Do I need RMB cash?+
Keep cash as a backup. Most places accept mobile payment, but cash is useful for small vendors and rural areas.
Q3: What if my card cannot be linked?+
Contact your bank to enable international transactions. Some banks require travel notification. Alternatively, use a different card or withdraw cash at an ATM.
Q4: Which payment app should I use?+
Both Alipay and WeChat Pay are widely accepted. Use WeChat Pay if you rely on WeChat for communication.
